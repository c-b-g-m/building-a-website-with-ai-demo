About us

A coffee shop on Yale Street. Opened in 2018. Still here.

How it started

The Heights had plenty of cafes by 2018. Most of them were trying to be six things at once — coffee, brunch, retail, co-working, event space. We wanted a place that did one thing well and closed by mid-afternoon.

So we leased a small bay between the post office and the cleaners. Painted the walls. Put in a bar. Started buying beans from a few roasters we trusted, and a few we wanted to try.

How we run it

We open at 6. We close at 2. We're closed on Mondays because the team has lives. Our menu is short on purpose — drip, pour-over, and one reserve we rotate every couple of weeks.

We don't do oat-milk turmeric anything. We're not against it. It's just not what we're good at.

The neighborhood

Most of our customers walk in. Some of them have been coming since the first month. We host two regulars-only events a month — a Friday-night coffee tasting and a Sunday-morning latte-art demo. If you've been in three times, you're invited.
